Project 3 Title

Project 3 Title reimagines the traditional Japanese lantern as an interactive audio sculpture, combining a multi-driver Bluetooth speaker with faceted translucent acrylic. Light, sound, and form come together to create an immersive desktop listening experience.

Overview

Project 3 Title is an interactive audio sculpture that reinterprets the traditional Japanese lantern through contemporary fabrication and embedded technology. A multi-driver Bluetooth speaker system is housed within a faceted translucent structure, allowing light and sound to coexist as a unified sensory experience.


The segmented acrylic form creates a rhythmic interplay of geometry and illumination, diffusing light while subtly revealing the internal architecture. An illuminated base enhances the object's spatial presence, introducing a responsive ambient layer that interacts with its environment.


Balancing acoustic function with sculptural intent, Project 3 Title explores the relationship between material, light, and sound in a compact desktop form. The result is an object that transforms everyday listening into an atmospheric and immersive experience.
