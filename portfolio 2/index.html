What is Creative Technology?

Creative Technology is engineering with a design mindset. Instead of only asking "does it work?", we ask "how should it look, feel, and fit into someone's life?" We solve real engineering problems in creative ways by combining fabrication, electronics, programming, and product design into one process: sketch it, model it, build it, break it, fix it.

Digital Fabrication

3D printing, laser cutting, and CNC machining turn digital models into physical parts.

Electronics

Soldering, wiring, and PCB design bring objects to life with light, sound, and sensors.

Product Development

Balancing function, materials, cost, and user experience with an entrepreneurial lens.
